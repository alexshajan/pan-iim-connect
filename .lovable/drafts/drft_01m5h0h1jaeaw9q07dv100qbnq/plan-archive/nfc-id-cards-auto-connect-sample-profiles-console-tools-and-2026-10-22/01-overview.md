# NFC ID cards: auto-connect, sample profiles, console tools and themed QR downloads

## What you get
1. **Tap or scan, and you're connected.** Opening a card link while signed in saves the cardholder to your network straight away, adds you to theirs too, buzzes the phone, and shows "Prof. Basav saved to your network!" Tap OK and you stay on their profile, where you can add a note. If you're signed out, you see the profile, a "Save contact" button and "Sign in to save". After sign-in the connection completes on its own.
2. **Committee and volunteer cards work right away.** They don't wait for a desk check-in. Delegate cards still need one.
3. **Four sample profiles**, all with a blank Submission ID: Ashish (Volunteer), Valay (Volunteer), Prof. Vinay (Steering Committee) and Prof. Basav (Co-Convener).
4. **Console tools for people:** add, edit and delete profiles. You can also tick people and export the vendor sheet.
5. **Themed QR codes** in pine green with rounded dots and soft rounded corner eyes. A small pine-cone mark sits in the middle, and the identifier code (e.g. `WMC-BAS1`) is printed in a pill below the QR so it's easy to check. The codes use high error correction, so they still scan with the mark in the middle.
6. **QR download per person:** a download button on each row gives a print-quality PNG and SVG. "Export for vendor" gives one ZIP with the sheet (S.No, Name, Role, Submission ID, Code, NFC URL, QR URL, QR file name) plus one QR file per person, named after their code.
7. **Card preview** of the front and back in the console, using the same QR. Emergency contacts are left blank.
