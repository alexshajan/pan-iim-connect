# Agenda v2: "My Agenda" / "Conference Programme" toggle

## What changes for the user
The Agenda page splits into two sides behind one switch at the top:

- **My Agenda**: only what concerns this person. That means their own paper slots, sessions they chair or review, keynotes they give, sessions they starred, and shared items like meals and plenaries.
- **Conference Programme**: the full timeline set by the committee. All days, all rooms and all tracks.

## Default screen: the calendar overview
My Agenda opens on a **3-day calendar overview** (27, 28, 29 Nov side by side, time running down). Each block is one of their commitments; empty stretches are shown as **free slots**. At a glance the user sees their whole conference and when they are free.

- **Tap a day header, or a "Day view" button**: drills down into that date (the date-wise view that exists today, filtered to them).
- **Swipe left/right in Day view**: moves to the previous or next day. Date pills stay at the top as the tap alternative.
- **Tap a free slot**: opens the Programme at that time, showing what's running so they can fill the gap.
- **Tap any block**: opens the pop-up with just their part: role, arrival time, room, co-presenters, directions.
- **Back / "Overview" button**: returns to the 3-day calendar.

The Programme side keeps its own views (Timeline, Rooms, List, Search) and opens on today's date.

| My Agenda | Conference Programme |
|---|---|
| Overview (3-day calendar, default) | Timeline (default) |
| Day view (swipe between days) | Rooms (room-by-time grid) |
| Clashes & gaps | List / Tracks |
| (Volunteers: My shifts) | Search |

The switch and last view are remembered. Signed-out visitors see only the Programme, with a "Sign in for your agenda" note.

## Why this improves the journey
- The first question a delegate has is "what am I doing and when am I free?" The overview answers it in one screen.
- Free slots become a direct path into the Programme, so discovery follows naturally.
- Swiping between days in Day view is quick on phones, while date pills keep it accessible.
- Feasible with what exists: sessions, stars and roles are already in place, and the day view reuses today's timeline.
